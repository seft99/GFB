## Editorial treatment

- Awkward or contradictory source copy was rewritten while preserving meaning.
- Pages distinguish supply, development, coordination and consulting; they do not state that GFB manufactures the full portfolio.
- Product dimensions, standards and technical claims are not repeated unless the source is clear; commercial confirmation is requested instead.
- No certifications, clients, testimonials, prices, delivery promises or sustainability guarantees were invented.
