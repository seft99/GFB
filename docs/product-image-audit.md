# Product image rights audit

Commercial-use and copyright traceability ledger for product-facing imagery on the GFB Solutions
website. This record is operational, not legal advice, and does not state that every possible legal
risk has been eliminated.

Last review: **2026-10-29**.

## Publication gate

No newly sourced image may be added to a production-facing component unless its row is
`verified-commercial` or GFB has supplied written authorization and the row is
`authorized-existing`.

Statuses:

- `verified-commercial`: the original asset page, copyright owner or creator, applicable licence,
  commercial-use permission and modification permission were verified. Remaining depicted-rights
  considerations are recorded.
- `generated-commercial`: generated without an input/reference image; the applicable output
  ownership terms were checked, the prompt and generation record are documented, and a human
  visual review found no visible person, brand, logo, artwork or protected product design. This is
  not a representation that the output is unique, copyrightable in every jurisdiction, or free of
  every possible third-party claim.
- `authorized-existing`: GFB has supplied written authorization or ownership evidence. The evidence
  location must be added to the row before this status is used.
- `pending-review`: permission, ownership or third-party rights are incomplete. Do not use this
  asset for a new integration, replacement or new production-facing placement.
- `rejected`: not used because the licence, depicted subject or product accuracy was unsuitable.

Public availability, prior publication on GFB's Wix site, attribution, or possession of a local
file is not proof of permission. `Royalty-free` is not treated as public domain. Editorial-only
assets are not acceptable for this corporate promotional website.

## Required workflow for future images

1. Start the row in this file before integrating the image.
2. Open the original asset page, not an image-search result or CDN-only URL.
3. Record creator/owner, licence name and URL, commercial permission, modification permission,
   attribution and depicted-rights observations.
4. Record the verification date and, where practical, preserve dated evidence supplied by the
   owner or licensing platform.
5. Use `pending-review` until all required evidence exists. Do not reference a `pending-review`
   image from a new production-facing component.
6. After integration, record every local derivative and route/scope, then update the completion
   report.
7. Re-check licences when replacing an asset or materially changing its use. Never fabricate a
   release, ownership record or legal approval.
